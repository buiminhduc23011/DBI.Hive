## 2026-02-01 - [Search Component Accessibility]
**Learning:** Custom search dropdowns using divs and buttons often lack semantic ARIA roles (combobox/listbox) and accessible labels for icon-only buttons (like Clear/Search).
**Action:** Always verify custom interactive components with ARIA roles and ensure all icon-only buttons have descriptive aria-labels.
\n## 2026-05-09 - [Dropdown Menu Accessibility]\n**Learning:** When creating custom dropdown menus using icon-only triggers (like MoreVertical), it's not enough to just add an `aria-label`. To properly inform screen readers of the interactive nature and state of the component, you must also add `aria-haspopup="true"` and bind `aria-expanded` to the menu's open state. Furthermore, decorative inner SVGs must be hidden with `aria-hidden="true"` to prevent confusing redundant announcements.\n**Action:** Always combine `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded`, and `aria-hidden` on the SVG when adding an `aria-label` to an icon-only dropdown trigger.
